Then Remove Old fc43 Kernels

After rebooting into the new kernel:

sudo dnf remove kernel-*fc43*

To View the Boot Entries

Your glob expansion failed because sudo doesn't expand *. Do this instead:

sudo bash -c 'cat /boot/loader/entries/*.conf'

Or:

sudo cat /boot/loader/entries/f8039b650e194ca7aea174a7c2e437b9-6.19.14-200.fc43.x86_64.conf

After Installing New Kernel

Reboot and you should see Fedora 44 with fc44 kernels in the boot menu.
